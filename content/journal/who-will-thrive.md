title: Who Will Thrive
date: 2026-11-02
category: personal
summary: A hypothesis about the kinds of people I think will do well in the years ahead.
---
I have a hypothesis about what the people who do well in the coming years will have in common.

**Artists.** Since I started working, I've spent a lot of time around artists, and I think believing AI will replace them is a mistake. Artists see things and understand reality in ways the rest of us often can't. That's what will keep them sharp, and what lets them find the angle nobody else is looking at.

**Hungry people.** Information is everywhere now. What's rare is the person who goes out of their way to find resources and actually acts on them. I think the people who go to extraordinary lengths to learn, to make things, and to make knowledge their own, and who point that ambition toward good, will be the outliers. I think they'll be the happy ones too.

**People who ask a lot of questions.** New ideas have always come from curiosity. AI can't create something new on its own; it always needs a human to ask. The people who rely on themselves to learn, build and create will lead the way, for themselves and for everyone around them.
