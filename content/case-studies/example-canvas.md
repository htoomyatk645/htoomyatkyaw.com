## The problem

Start with the situation in two or three short paragraphs. Who was it for, what was going wrong, and what did you need to change?

This example uses one slide from a Briefly carousel, because the files were already in the project. Replace it with your own work.

## How I thought about it

Use ordinary text between boards to explain your reasoning. Short paragraphs work best.

:::decision Decision
Use a decision block for a choice you made and the reason for it.
:::

- A list for options you considered
- Or for constraints you worked within
