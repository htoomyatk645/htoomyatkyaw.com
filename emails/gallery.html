Email kit

Eighteen emails in the look of htoomyatkyaw.com. Pick one, fill in the blanks, and copy it into Gmail or any other mail app.

Subject

"Copy email" keeps the design when you paste into a new Gmail message. Blanks you leave empty stay as {{name}} so you can spot them. The heading picture and portrait load from htoomyatkyaw.com, so they appear in sent mail once the site is deployed.
